Pathira

Competitive product roadmap

Build the workflow tool people can trust.

A prioritized checklist based on recurring complaints about Tango, Scribe, Supademo, iorad, Folge, UserGuiding, and similar process-capture products.

The strategic wedge

Win on capture reliability, editing depth, privacy, portability, and transparent pricing—not automatic screenshots alone.

Already working

Persistent side panel, cross-tab click capture, ordered screenshots, page context, guide editing, local storage, and a Chrome Store-ready package.

Biggest market gap

Users cannot trust silent capture failures, dislike cleanup-heavy output, and fear losing data behind expensive or closed platforms.

Reliability

Never lose or silently skip a step.

Editing

Turn raw capture into polished instructions quickly.

Ownership

Keep projects portable, private, and recoverable.

Sharing

Let viewers follow guides without friction.

Pathira Product Improvement Checklist1 / 6
P0 · BUILD NEXT

Capture trust

Make recording impossible to doubt.

Persistent side panel

Keep the live timeline visible while moving between tabs.

Ordered screenshots and context

Store every click with image, label, page title, URL, and coordinates.

Checkpoint every event

Persist each step immediately instead of waiting for capture completion.

Recover interrupted sessions

Resume or finalize after an extension, tab, or browser crash.

Visible capture errors

Show a clear warning when a click or screenshot fails.

Reliable pause and resume

Test switching tabs, windows, and long page loads.

Noise suppression

Detect duplicate, accidental, and low-value clicks before import.

Manual “Capture this step”

Save a screenshot without requiring a click.

Screenshot timing control

Choose pre-click, post-click, or delayed capture.

Autosave and quota warnings

Prevent browser storage limits from destroying work.

Reliability before AI2 / 6
P0 · CORE EDITING

Editing and privacy

Make every captured step fixable.

Undo and redo

Cover capture deletion, ordering, text, and image edits.

Complete screenshot editor

Crop, zoom, blur, redact, draw arrows, add boxes, and place text.

Edit click targets

Move, resize, recolor, or remove the click highlight.

Replace screenshots

Swap one image without rerecording the workflow.

Bulk step operations

Select, delete, merge, duplicate, or reorder many steps.

Encrypted local database

Replace oversized localStorage records with durable project storage.

On-device PII redaction

Detect sensitive text and blur it before cloud transmission.

Domain allowlists

Let teams define where capture is permitted or blocked.

Sensitive-field warnings

Warn near password, payment, health, and identity fields.

Permanent deletion controls

Delete one capture, one guide, or all stored data.

Editing is the product, not cleanup tax3 / 6
P1 · MAINTENANCE

Guide structure

Help guides stay useful as work changes.

Sections and reusable blocks

Add headings, callouts, warnings, and shared content.

Merge and split guides

Combine captures or divide one long process.

Conditional branches

Document multiple valid paths in one guide.

Variables

Reuse names, environments, and customer-specific values.

Templates

Start from SOP, onboarding, troubleshooting, and support patterns.

Folders, tags, and search

Organize large libraries without enterprise-only restrictions.

Version history and restore

Show visual differences and recover prior releases.

Stale-guide detection

Flag likely UI changes, broken links, and missing elements.

Recapture one step

Refresh one instruction instead of repeating everything.

Portable project ZIP

Include original and edited images, HTML, Markdown, JSON, and a manifest.

Maintenance determines long-term value4 / 6
P1 · DISTRIBUTION

Sharing and business model

Remove friction for viewers and teams.

Cloud-backed share links

Replace browser-local links with durable publishing.

Account-free viewing

Never force recipients to register before following a guide.

Sharing controls

Add passwords, expiration dates, and email-domain restrictions.

Knowledge-base embeds

Support Notion, Confluence, Hudu, Zendesk, and help centers.

Comments and outdated-step reports

Let viewers identify confusing or stale instructions.

Free viewers

Charge creators or workspace usage—not every recipient.

Mixed workspace roles

Allow free and paid roles together.

Transparent checkout

Show full monthly, annual, and renewal costs before purchase.

Self-service billing

Support upgrades, downgrades, cancellation, and refunds.

Custom branding

Do not hide basic brand control behind an enterprise contract.

Viewer access should increase distribution5 / 6
P2 · DIFFERENTIATION

Expansion roadmap

Scale only after trust is earned.

Capture expansion

Desktop applications, voice narration, optional video, translation, and in-app Guide Me playback.

Learning layer

Completion analytics, drop-off analysis, training assignments, due dates, and reminders.

Accessibility

Keyboard-only playback, alt text, semantic exports, and contrast checks.

Developer platform

API, webhooks, integrations, and MCP access.

Controlled AI

Audience-aware rewriting, confidence indicators, original evidence, and batch approval.

Optional cloud

Encrypted synchronization that never removes local ownership.

Next sprint recommendation

Build event checkpoints, pause/resume recovery, visible error states, accidental-click filtering, manual capture, and undo/redo before adding more AI.
